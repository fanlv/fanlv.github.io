隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

一签（OneSlip，以下简称「本 App」）尊重并保护你的隐私。本政策说明本 App 如何处理与你有关的信息。

一、我们不收集个人信息

本 App 无需注册或登录，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、精确位置、通讯录、相册、设备标识符等信息，也不包含第三方广告或数据分析 SDK。

二、保存在你设备上的数据

你抽到的签文记录、每日提醒时间、真太阳时经度设置，仅保存在你设备的本地存储中（UserDefaults）。卸载 App 后这些数据随之删除，开发者无法访问。

三、通知

若你开启「每日提醒」，本 App 会请求通知权限，并在本地按你设定的时间发送提醒。提醒内容由设备本地生成，不经过任何服务器。你可以随时在 App 设置或系统设置中关闭。

四、网络与第三方

当前版本不发起任何网络请求，不向任何第三方传输或共享数据。若未来引入需要联网的功能，我们会在本页面更新说明，并在 App 内征得你的同意。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。若你认为我们在未获同意的情况下接触了儿童信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快处理。

六、内容性质说明

本 App 的签文内容源于传统民俗整理，仅供娱乐与自省参考，不构成医疗、投资、法律或其他专业建议。

七、政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新内容并同步修改生效日期。重大变更会在 App 内提示。

八、联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，可通过 App Store 产品页上所列的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

OneSlip ("the App") respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information related to you.

1. We do not collect personal information

The App requires no account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and the App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your drawn slips, daily reminder time, and true-solar-time longitude setting are stored only in local storage on your device (UserDefaults). Uninstalling the App removes this data. The developer cannot access it.

3. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the App requests notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. Reminder content is generated on-device and never passes through a server. You can turn it off at any time in the App or in system settings.

4. Network and third parties

The current version makes no network requests and shares no data with third parties. If a future version introduces online features, we will update this page and ask for your consent in the App.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has reached us without consent, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

6. Nature of the content

Slip texts are compiled from traditional folk material and are provided for entertainment and self-reflection only. They are not medical, financial, legal, or other professional advice.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please reach us through the developer contact information listed on our App Store product page.
