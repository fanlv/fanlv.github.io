隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

适用范围。本政策适用于本开发者（下称「我们」）在 App Store 上发布的所有 App。部分 App 会在本政策基础上另有说明，如有冲突，以该 App 内的说明为准。

一、我们不建立用户画像

我们的 App 不要求注册或登录，不出售、不出租、不交换任何用户信息，也不使用你的数据做广告投放或跨 App 追踪。

二、我们收集的信息

默认情况下，我们的 App 不收集可识别个人身份的信息，例如姓名、手机号、邮箱、精确位置、通讯录、相册或设备标识符。

如果某个 App 需要联网、需要访问系统权限（如相册、麦克风、通知、定位），或需要由你主动提交内容，我们会在该 App 首次使用相关功能时说明用途，并只在获得你同意后使用。该 App 的具体数据实践以其 App 内说明为准。

三、保存在你设备上的数据

App 的设置、记录等通常保存在你设备的本地存储中。卸载 App 后这些数据会随之删除，我们无法远程读取或恢复。

ClashVerge：代理连接与本机配置

ClashVerge 是使用自有节点的代理客户端，不提供服务器、账号或订阅。开发者不收集、出售、出租、使用或向第三方披露你的使用数据，不保存浏览历史、访问网址、流量内容或请求日志，也不使用统计、广告或追踪服务。

节点凭据、订阅地址、套餐快照和设置保存在本机系统钥匙串中，不上传给开发者。钥匙串项目可能在卸载后继续保留；你可在 App 中删除节点和订阅。我们无法远程读取或恢复这些配置。

启用 VPN 后，流量依照你选择的节点与规则直连、转发或拦截；你选择的代理及 DNS 提供方会处理提供连接所必需的流量与查询。默认 DNS 是 Cloudflare 1.1.1.1，可在设置中更改。开发者不运营代理服务；请自行选择可信提供方。

只有你主动操作时，App 才会下载你指定的 HTTPS 订阅、从 GitHub 的 pexcn/daily 下载推荐 GFWList，或直接测试节点 TCP 连通性；对应服务会接收连接所需的 IP 地址等标准网络信息。二维码相机画面仅在本机处理，不保存、不上传。App 不安装根证书、不解密 HTTPS，也不自动读取剪贴板。

这些用途会在连接所在的首页说明。使用帮助与支持；邮箱：feedback8app@gmail.com。

Veil：规则更新与网页处理

Veil 的网页配色、元素隐藏、站点设置与自定义规则均在设备上处理，不上传访问过的网址、浏览历史、页面内容或自定义规则。内置规则离线可用。

只有你主动检查更新或开启自动更新时，Veil 才会从 apple.fanlv.fun/veil/rules/ 下载预编译规则包。自动检查在打开 App 或系统允许后台刷新时执行；可随时关闭。下载使用普通 HTTPS 请求，托管服务会接收连接所需的标准网络信息（例如 IP 地址），不会收到你的浏览记录。Veil 不将这些请求用于统计、广告或追踪。规则来源、日期、作者和许可证在 App 设置及规则署名文件中列出。更新失败时保留原有规则。

Veil 支持邮箱：feedback8app@gmail.com。

四、通知

若某个 App 提供提醒功能并要求通知权限，提醒由你的设备在本地生成和发送，不经过我们的服务器。你可以随时在 App 内或系统设置中关闭。

五、第三方服务

我们的 App 默认不集成第三方广告或数据分析 SDK。若某个 App 使用了第三方服务（如崩溃统计、云同步），我们会在该 App 的说明或 App Store 隐私标签中列明服务提供方及涉及的信息类型。

六、数据安全

我们采取合理的技术措施保护数据。由于 App 默认不在服务器端保存你的个人信息，主要风险来自设备本身，建议你开启设备密码与备份。

七、儿童隐私

我们的 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集个人信息。若你认为我们在未获监护人同意的情况下接触了儿童信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快删除。

八、你的权利

你可以随时卸载 App 以停止使用并删除本地数据。若你所在地区的法律赋予你访问、更正、删除或导出个人信息的权利，可通过下方方式联系我们。

九、政策更新

本政策如有变更，我们会在本页面更新并修改生效日期。重大变更会在相关 App 内提示。

十、联系我们

对本政策有疑问，或需要行使上述权利，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们，我们通常在几个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Scope. This policy applies to all apps we publish on the App Store. Some apps may add their own notes on top of this policy; where they conflict, the in-app notice for that app controls.

1. No profiling

Our apps do not require an account. We do not sell, rent, or trade user information, and we do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. Information we collect

By default our apps do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

If an app needs a network connection, a system permission (photos, microphone, notifications, location), or content you choose to submit, we explain the purpose the first time you use that feature and only proceed with your consent. That app's in-app notice describes its specific practices.

3. Data stored on your device

Settings and records are typically stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We cannot read or restore it remotely.

ClashVerge: proxy connections and local configuration

ClashVerge is a bring-your-own-server proxy client. It supplies no servers, accounts, or subscriptions. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, use, or disclose your usage data to third parties. We keep no browsing history, visited URLs, traffic contents, or request logs, and use no analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Server credentials, subscription addresses, plan snapshots, and settings are stored in the system keychain on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. Keychain items may remain after uninstalling the app. You can remove servers and subscriptions in the app. We cannot read or restore these configurations remotely.

While VPN is enabled, traffic goes direct, through your chosen proxy, or is blocked according to your rules. Your chosen proxy and DNS operators process traffic and queries needed to provide their services. DNS defaults to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 and can be changed in Settings. The developer does not operate the proxy service; choose providers you trust.

Only your requested actions download your HTTPS subscriptions, fetch the recommended GFWList from pexcn/daily on GitHub, or test TCP connectivity directly to your servers. Those services receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Camera images used to scan QR codes are processed on-device, without saving or uploading. The app installs no root certificates, does not decrypt HTTPS, and does not read the clipboard automatically.

The Home screen explains these purposes before connection. Help and support; email: feedback8app@gmail.com.

Veil: rule updates and webpage processing

Veil processes webpage colors, element hiding, site settings, and custom rules on your device. It does not upload visited URLs, browsing history, page contents, or custom rules. Bundled rules work offline.

Only when you check for updates or enable automatic updates does Veil download precompiled rule packages from apple.fanlv.fun/veil/rules/. Automatic checks run when you open the app or when iOS permits background refresh; you can turn them off. Downloads use normal HTTPS requests, so the hosting service receives standard connection information such as an IP address, but no browsing history. Veil does not use these requests for analytics, advertising, or tracking. Sources, dates, authors, and licenses are listed in the app and the rule attribution notice. Failed updates preserve previous rules.

Veil support: feedback8app@gmail.com.

4. Notifications

If an app offers reminders and asks for notification permission, reminders are generated and delivered locally by your device and do not pass through our servers. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in system settings.

5. Third-party services

Our apps do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs by default. If an app uses a third-party service such as crash reporting or cloud sync, we list the provider and the data involved in that app's notes or in its App Store privacy details.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect data. Because our apps do not store your personal information on our servers by default, the main risk sits with the device itself — please use a passcode and backups.

7. Children's privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information reached us without a guardian's consent, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your rights

You can uninstall an app at any time to stop using it and delete local data. If your local law grants you rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, contact us using the method below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the affected apps.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or to exercise the rights above, reach us through GitHub Issues. We usually reply within a few business days.
